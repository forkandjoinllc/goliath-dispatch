## Notes

- **Tenant isolation demo:** both tenants have an independent carrier record named
  "Rivera Transport LLC" with the same USDOT number — two fully separate rows,
  never shared or linked, demonstrating that identical business identity in two
  tenants stays completely isolated (in tenant A it is approved-then-suspended; in
  tenant B it is approved and active).
- The Accounting user in tenant A has TOTP MFA enrolled and confirmed through the
  real enrollment path (`beginMfaEnrollment`/`confirmMfaEnrollment`) — sign in, then
  use any TOTP app against the same secret, or the account's recovery codes.
- Every seeded record was created through the real service layer (`createCarrier`,
  `createLoad`, `transitionStatus`, `sendInvoice`, `signDocument`, …), not direct
  database inserts, except where explicitly commented otherwise in the seed source.
